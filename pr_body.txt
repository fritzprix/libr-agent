💡 **What**
Added an `aria-label` to the `Switch` component in the `ScheduledTaskRow`.

🎯 **Why**
The switch toggles the enabled state of a scheduled task, but lacked an accessible name, making it invisible to screen readers.

📸 **Before/After**
No visual change; purely semantic accessibility enhancement.

♿ **Accessibility**
Screen reader users can now understand the purpose and target of the switch component (e.g., 'Toggle active state for Backup Task').
